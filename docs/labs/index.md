# Labs

Ten labs, one 90-minute session per Wednesday, building a four-motor autonomous
car and then a coordinated fleet. Dates are on the [Schedule](../schedule.md).

## Before every lab

- Read the lab page before you arrive. Sessions are 90 minutes.
- Bring a laptop with the Arduino IDE working. See [Setup](../resources/setup.md).
- Bring your group's car, battery charged.
- Bring your pin table from [Lab 1](lab01-pwm-motors.md#record-your-connections). Every later lab assumes it.
- Know where the fire blanket and the LiPo bucket are. See [Safety](../resources/safety.md).
